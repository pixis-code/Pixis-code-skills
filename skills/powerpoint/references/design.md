# Deck design

Decide once, before any slide: outline, 1–3 fonts, and a hex palette. Reuse them on every slide.

## Palette

One dark, one light, and 1–2 accents. No pure `#FFFFFF` or `#000000`. Two light backgrounds may alternate, plus one dark statement slide. Body text needs 4.5:1 contrast (3:1 at 44px and up). Colors that must be distinguished differ in lightness, not only hue. A label carries the meaning, not color alone.

Named themes are starts only. `palette` or `colors` replaces them when the user names colors.

## Type

One scale: 48 / 36 / 22 / 16 / 12 pt. Nothing below 12. Emphasize with weight or color. Serif titles, sans body, and a different pairing on the next deck. `fonts.title` and `fonts.body` set the pair. `fonts.font` sets both.

## Layout and content

16:9. The heading stays on the same top line. Slides of the same kind share a structure. Footer is one row: label left, page number right. Fill about 70% of the column. Card text is centered, and card height follows the text. One idea per slide: cards, a table, big numbers, or a statement. The key card may be `"tone": "dark"`. Titles share one grammar. Do not invent numbers or quotes; use `[Presenter name]`.

Build an index, then the cover, then slides in small batches. Every slide has speaker notes.
